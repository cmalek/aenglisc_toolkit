Quickstart
==========

This page assumes you've already followed :doc:`/overview/installation`.

Five minutes to your first annotation
---------------------------------------

1. Launch the app.
2. **File → New Project...** (**Ctrl+N**). Give it a title and
   (optionally) a source.
3. Paste or type some Old English text into the project.
4. Select a word and press **Enter** (or double-click it) to open the
   annotation modal.
5. Press a letter key to pick a part of speech (**N** for noun, **V** for
   verb, etc.), fill in what you're confident about, and press **Enter**
   to apply.
6. Type a Modern English rendering of the sentence into its translation
   field.

That's the whole loop: annotate a word, translate a sentence, repeat. You
don't need to fill in every annotation field on the first pass — see
:doc:`/overview/annotation` for the incremental workflow.

.. tip::

   Press **F1** at any time to open the in-app Help Center — it covers
   every workflow in more depth than these docs, and is always in sync
   with the version of the app you're running.
